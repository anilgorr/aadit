# Unsourced Statistics and Time-Sensitive Claims

Audit date: 28 August 2026

## Method

All 33 post source files were scanned for numerical, statistical, financial, market-size, salary, pricing, incident-volume, and time-sensitive regulatory claims. None of the post files contains an inline `http://` or `https://` source link. The inventory below records claims that require a source or human verification; no citations were added or invented.

Frontmatter dates, numbered list labels, framework names, product version names, and routine descriptions such as “24/7 monitoring” were excluded unless they formed a substantive quantitative claim.

## Stale-content findings

- The TCS share-price line dated 29 October 2021 was unrelated to the SOC-provider discussion and has been removed.
- `top-soc-service-providers-in-india-secure-your-business.mdx` predicts continued emphasis “in 2023”; this is stale forecast language and remains flagged for editorial review.
- `top-cyber-security-companies-in-india-safeguarding-digital-future.mdx` says “The date is October 2023” while discussing the industry in 2025; the temporal framing is internally inconsistent.
- `cyber-security-in-india-trends-challenges-growth.mdx` uses “by 2025” and “this year” for forecasts that are now historical and conflicts with its newer 2025–2030 forecast.
- The 2025 draft DPDP Rules are described without a current official-status source. Regulatory status must be re-verified before editorial updating.
- The bulk `updatedAt: 2026-07-02` value is treated by Velite as a migration date, not an editorial freshness signal.

## Human-input dependency

The flagged claims need source URLs supplied or approved by the site owner. Until then, they should remain flagged, be softened to non-numeric wording, or be removed in a separate editorial review.
