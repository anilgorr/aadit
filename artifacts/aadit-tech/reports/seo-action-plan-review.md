Search-volume, ranking, click and AI-citation numbers in the workbook are **unverified historical baselines**; no current Search Console or Bing data was available.

## Phase 1 — technical fixes

| Workbook item | Result |
| --- | --- |
| 1–2. Broken title/description truncation | The shared metadata helper preserves authored text (no ellipses or padding). The original crawl reported 32 titles over 65 characters, 40 descriptions over 160, and 10 under 120; a fresh 82-route preview crawl before this editorial pass found 31, 35, and 11 respectively. Page-specific edits now bring all **non-priority** rendered titles to at most 65 characters and descriptions to 120–160 characters, without restoring automatic truncation. This includes service, blog, hub, glossary, author, and static-page metadata; the long HIPAA service H1 remains visible while its shorter authored title supplies search metadata. A post-edit crawl of 82 sitemap routes found only seven affected routes, all in the eight-post approval-gated set: six titles over 65, one description over 160, and three descriptions under 120. The eight proposals in `metadata-review.md` remain **unapplied pending explicit owner approval**; some proposals retain titles or descriptions outside these numeric targets by editorial choice, so review them before approval. |
| 3. Duplicate trailing slashes | Canonicals already preferred no trailing slash. Changed routing to issue 301 to the no-slash path; a local `/cybersecurity/vapt/` request now returns 301 to `/cybersecurity/vapt`. The 15 examples claimed in the workbook were not enumerated, so the general rule covers them. |
| 4. Double redirects | The original 66 known legacy rules issue one-hop 301 responses for both slash and non-slash source URLs instead of an intermediate Next.js 308; the approved SOC article redirect adds one more. Previously checked 132 original source forms with zero failures. Rechecked wildcard `/category/`, `/tag/`, `/author/`, `/ebook/`, `/whitepaper/`, plus a migrated blog slug after correcting wildcard matching. Canonical host and retired-domain redirects also return 301 locally. |
| 5. Complete pre-migration inventory | **Open.** The repository has a partial redirect inventory, but the complete historical URL export (and the workbook's four confirmed traffic-bearing URLs) was not provided. Obtain it from the previous site/developer and compare against the redirect map and Search Console before claiming migration coverage. |
