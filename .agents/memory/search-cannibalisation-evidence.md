---
name: Search cannibalisation evidence
description: Evidence standard for deciding whether to consolidate live Aadit search URLs.
---

Do not treat separately aggregated Search Console Pages and Queries exports as a query-to-URL mapping. A manually compiled keyword-to-page workbook can establish overlap for its listed examples, but not an unlisted site-wide count; distinguish it from the raw export. Request a complete combined `query` + `page` export for the same date range, or filter a priority query and export its Pages tab, before claiming comprehensive overlap or proposing broad consolidation.

**Why:** The owner first supplied aggregate exports that showed meaningful SOC impressions on several live URLs, but no shared query/page dimension. A later workbook compiled URL-level examples for 20 queries while asserting a larger count without listing its underlying rows. Redirecting high-exposure pages based on incomplete examples could lose distinct search intent or useful URLs.

**How to apply:** For SOC and other competing-page decisions, preserve pages with distinct intent unless a source-supported overlap and explicit owner approval justify a targeted 301. Keep one-hop redirects and check the sitemap, internal links and content exports when a URL becomes noncanonical.
