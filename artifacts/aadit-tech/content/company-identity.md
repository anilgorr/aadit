# Aadit Technologies

> Aadit Technologies Pvt. Ltd. (CIN U72900KA2017PTC099151) is a cybersecurity,
> compliance, and IT managed-services company founded in Bengaluru, India on 12
> January 2017. We serve startups through enterprises in healthcare, BFSI,
> fintech, e-commerce, and SaaS, with offices in Bengaluru, Bellevue, and Dubai.
> Aadit Technologies is not affiliated with Aadit Techno Solutions
> (aadittech.com), a defence electronics company.

## Key facts

- Founded: 12 January 2017 by Guruprasad N. Joshi
- Leadership: Anil Gorraladaku (Chief Executive Officer, since 2022); Srinivas Gadicherla (Chief Technology Officer)
- Headquarters: #21 & 22, AnandAM, 4th Main Road, 3rd Block, Opposite Axis Bank, New BEL Road, Bengaluru 560094, Karnataka, India
- Frameworks supported: ISO 27001, ISO 42001, ISO 9001, SOC 1, SOC 2, HIPAA, PCI DSS, GDPR, India DPDP Act, RBI cybersecurity guidelines, and CSCRF
